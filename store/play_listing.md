## App name (30 characters max)
Music Player

## Short description (80 characters max)
A fast, private player for the music already on your phone. No ads, no account.

## Full description
Music Player plays the songs stored on your phone, and nothing else. No account, no ads, no tracking, and it works fully offline.

LIBRARY
• Browse by songs, albums and artists, and search your whole library
• Playlists with your own cover images, reorderable by dragging
• Liked Songs, Recently Played and On Repeat, which learns the songs you really listen to

NOW PLAYING
• Colours that follow the album art
• Swipe the cover to skip, drag down to close
• Queue, play next and several seek bar styles

SOUND
• Equalizer with presets, bass boost and virtualizer
• Playback speed and pitch
• Sleep timer, including "end of song"

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your music never leaves your device
• Optional tag clean-up looks up titles, artists and albums on MusicBrainz and changes a file only after you approve it

MAKE IT YOURS
• Light, dark or follow-device theme
• Choose your app icon

## Data safety form (Play Console)
- Does the app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No.** Nothing is sent to the developer or any developer-run server.
- The optional tag lookup sends a song's title and artist to MusicBrainz to get a result. Data sent only to answer the user's own request, and not stored by the developer, is generally not declared as "collected"; check the current Play wording for "ephemeral processing" before submitting.
- Is data encrypted in transit? **Yes** (HTTPS only).
- Can users request data deletion? Not applicable: no data is held by the developer. Local data is removed by clearing storage or uninstalling.

## Permission declarations
- `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO`: core function, playing the user's own music.
- `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`: background music playback.
- `WRITE_SETTINGS`: only for "Set as ringtone". Play reviews this permission closely; remove the ringtone feature and this permission if you don't want to justify it.
- `INTERNET`: optional tag lookup only.
